Why is Saving Water Important?

Only a very small percentage of the water on Earth is fresh drinking water. Here is why we must protect it:

  • Freshwater Scarcity: Clean water is limited and millions of people lack access to it.
  • Protects Wildlife: Saving water keeps rivers and lakes full, protecting animals and plants.
  • Saves Energy: Pumping and treating water takes a lot of electricity. Using less water saves energy too.
  • Saves Money: Lower water use means smaller utility bills for your household.

Easy Ways to Save Water

You can start saving water right now by following these simple tips:

  • Turn off the tap while brushing your teeth.
  • Take shorter showers instead of baths.
  • Fix leaking faucets and pipes immediately.
  • Only run the washing machine and dishwasher when they are full.
  • Water your garden in the morning or evening to stop it from evaporating quickly.

Interesting Water Facts

Here are some facts you should know about water:

  • About 71 percent of the Earth's surface is covered with water.
  • Only 2.5 percent of the Earth's water is freshwater.
  • Agriculture uses about 70 percent of all global freshwater.
  • A dripping faucet can waste dozens of gallons of water a month.
